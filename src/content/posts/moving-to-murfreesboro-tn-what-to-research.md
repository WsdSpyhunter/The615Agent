If you are moving to Murfreesboro TN, a few pieces of research will save you surprises later. Some of it is market data. Some of it depends on the exact address, like taxes and school zoning. And some of it is just the money and logistics that come with any move.

Here is the list I would work through before making an offer.

## Start with local numbers, not national headlines

![Photo: House keys, key, security, door key, shut down](/img/blog/moving-to-murfreesboro-tn-what-to-research/inline-1.jpg)

*Photo by [Schluesseldienst](https://pixabay.com/users/Schluesseldienst-13794927/) on [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com)*

Murfreesboro is in Rutherford County. It is not in Williamson County, so county services, taxes and schools follow Rutherford County rules.

Conditions can differ a lot from city to city, so look at Murfreesboro's own data. According to Redfin (rolling 3 months, data as of 2026-09-30), Murfreesboro's median sale price was $428,022, down 2.0% from a year earlier. Median days on market was 65, and the average sale-to-list ratio was 98.9%.

Redfin also showed 897 active listings and 4.3 months of supply. Months of supply is how long it would take to sell every active listing at the current pace of sales. Under 4 months favors sellers, 4 to 6 months is balanced, and over 6 months favors buyers. At 4.3, that figure sits in the balanced range, near the line with seller-favoring territory.

For a wider view, Redfin reported that the Nashville metro area had 5.7 months of supply for the 4 weeks ending 2026-10-25, with a median sale price of $464,333. Those are two different markets, and one number does not describe the other. You can also see the [Market data for the I-65 corridor](/market-data) for more context.

None of this predicts where prices will go. It is a snapshot of how things looked on those dates.

## Property taxes and schools depend on the address

Taxes are one of the first things people ask about. For the 2026 tax year, inside Murfreesboro city limits, the combined rate is $2.2414 per $100 of assessed value. That is $1.4885 for the county and $0.7529 for the city. Tennessee assesses residential property at 25% of appraised value, and the rate applies to that assessed amount.

Those are the 2026 rates after the countywide reappraisal, which lowered both. The county rate was $1.8762 before and the city rate was $0.9526. The source is Rutherford County: Commission approves budget (06/22/2026). The city rate is the certified rate presented to the City Council on July 2, 2026, so confirm the final adopted figure with the City of Murfreesboro. A home outside city limits will not carry the city portion, so ask about the exact address and check with [Rutherford County Government](https://www.rutherfordcountytn.gov).

Schools work the same way. Depending on the address, Murfreesboro students attend Murfreesboro City Schools, a district for pre-kindergarten through sixth grade, or Rutherford County Schools. The most reliable way to find the zoned school for a home is to ask each district. Start with [Rutherford County Schools](https://www.rcschools.net), and see the [Murfreesboro school districts](/murfreesboro/schools) page for how the two districts fit together.

I don't rate schools or neighborhoods. Do that check yourself for any home you are serious about.

## Look at commute and everyday costs

![Photo: House, garage, driveway, architecture, building](/img/blog/moving-to-murfreesboro-tn-what-to-research/inline-2.jpg)

*Photo by [Pexels](https://pixabay.com/users/Pexels-2286921/) on [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com)*

The U.S. Census Bureau's American Community Survey 5-year estimates (2020 to 2024) put Murfreesboro's mean travel time to work at 26.7 minutes. That is an average across the city, so your own drive could be quite different. Test the route from a specific address at the hours you would actually drive it. If a home is near MTSU or another busy area, visit at different times of day. [TDOT SmartWay traffic](https://smartway.tn.gov) is useful for checking current road conditions.

The same Census survey lists median household income at $80,108, median gross rent at $1,481 per month, and 52.4% of homes as owner-occupied. The rent figure is handy if you are weighing buying against renting. One way to frame that question is to compare the full monthly cost of buying (payment, taxes, insurance, maintenance) with the monthly cost of renting a comparable home, and to think about how long you expect to stay.

For more detail, see [Murfreesboro commute times](/murfreesboro/commute) and [Murfreesboro cost of living](/murfreesboro/cost-of-living). Utilities also depend on the address, so check with the provider that serves the home, such as [Middle Tennessee Electric](https://www.mte.com) if it applies.

## Get the money side in order

The Freddie Mac 30-year fixed rate was 7.24% for the week of 2026-10-29 (via FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis). Nobody can reliably predict where rates will go, so waiting for them to fall is a bet in either direction. The cost of waiting, such as rent paid or a home you wanted going to someone else, is real even if it is easy to overlook.

A few basics:

- Pre-approval is different from pre-qualification. Pre-approval means a lender has reviewed your documents and credit. It is not a final loan commitment, since final approval depends on the appraisal, underwriting and your finances staying the same until closing.
- Closing costs typically run 2 to 4 percent of the purchase price, plus earnest money and moving costs.
- After you apply, a lender must give you a Loan Estimate within three business days. The CFPB encourages getting Loan Estimates from more than one lender and comparing them, because rates and fees can differ.
- Avoid opening new credit, financing furniture or changing jobs from the time you apply through closing.

You choose your own lender. You can ask the Tennessee Housing Development Agency's participating lenders about THDA programs too. To try out a payment, use the [Mortgage calculator](/mortgage-calculator).

## Plan for inspections and the move itself

In Tennessee, sellers complete the residential property condition disclosure (or an exemption). The lead-based paint disclosure applies to homes built before 1978. Termite and wood-destroying organism (WDO) inspections are commonly requested by buyers and lenders. Radon testing and a sewer scope are common add-ons to a standard home inspection.

On moving day, request quotes from at least 3 licensed moving companies and book early for month-end dates. Verify any wire instructions by phone, using a number you already trust, and never rely on emailed instructions. Keep your old homeowner's insurance in place until closing on a sold home is confirmed.
