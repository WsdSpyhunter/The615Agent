If you are watching the Thompson's Station TN housing market, the numbers come from several places, and they do not always line up. Redfin, Zillow and Realtor.com each measure things a little differently.

Here is what each source shows right now, with dates, and how to read the numbers without overreading them. You can also see the town's figures on the [Thompson's Station housing market data](/thompsons-station/housing-market) page.

## What Redfin shows for Thompson's Station

![Photo: Home, for sale, house, estate, sale](/img/blog/thompsons-station-tn-housing-market-update/inline-1.jpg)

*Photo by [paulbr75](https://pixabay.com/users/paulbr75-2938186/) on [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com)*

City-level numbers come from the [Redfin Data Center](https://www.redfin.com/news/data-center/). For Thompson's Station, Redfin's rolling 3-month figures as of September 30, 2026 are:

- Median sale price: $833,246, up 1.7% from a year earlier
- Median days on market: 65
- Average sale-to-list ratio: 99.0%
- Active listings: 151
- Months of supply: 5.8

Sale-to-list is the sale price compared with the list price. At 99.0%, the average home in this window sold for about one percent under its list price.

Months of supply is how long it would take to sell every active listing at the current pace of sales. A common guide is that under 4 months favors sellers, 4 to 6 months is balanced, and over 6 months favors buyers. At 5.8, Thompson's Station sits in the balanced range, just under the line for buyers.

## How it compares with the Nashville metro

For context, Redfin reports the following for the Nashville metro area for the 4 weeks ending November 1, 2026:

- Median sale price: $468,307
- Median days on market: 68
- Sale-to-list: 97.6%
- Months of supply: 5.9
- Homes sold: 2,213

A few cautions. The Thompson's Station figures cover a rolling 3 months ending September 30. The metro figures cover 4 weeks ending November 1. The time windows differ, and the town figure for sale-to-list is an average, so treat the comparison as a rough one.

Still, the pattern is worth noting. Months of supply and days on market are close between the two. The median sale price is not. Conditions can differ a lot between cities, price ranges and neighborhoods, which is why local data tells you more than a national headline. For a wider view, see the [Nashville housing market data](/nashville/housing-market) page.

## Reading the numbers without overreading them

None of these numbers predict what prices or rates will do next. They describe recent sales and current listings.

A few ways to use them:

- **Days on market** shows how long homes took to sell. A median of 65 is a town-wide middle point. Individual homes can sell faster or slower depending on price, condition and location.
- **Sale-to-list** gives a sense of how much room there has been between asking and closing prices, on average.
- **Months of supply** shows how much inventory there is relative to the pace of sales.

Price range matters too. A town-wide median blends many types of homes, so the conditions for the home you care about may differ.

## What this may mean if you are buying or selling

This is general information, not advice.

If you are buying, the 30-year fixed mortgage rate was 7.18% for the week of November 5, 2026, according to Freddie Mac via FRED. Nobody can reliably predict where rates will go, so waiting for a lower rate is a bet in either direction. Many buyers focus on readiness instead: stable income, an emergency fund after closing, and a payment that fits comfortably. You can test payments with the [mortgage calculator](/mortgage-calculator).

Buyers can also ask about negotiating, such as asking a seller to cover some closing costs or fund a rate buydown. Whether that is available depends on the seller, the home and the market, and a lender must confirm what is allowed for your loan. Closing costs typically run 2 to 4 percent of the purchase price, plus earnest money and moving costs.

If you are selling, pricing is the main decision your market data can inform. Look at recent sales for homes like yours rather than the town-wide median. Days on market and sale-to-list show how the town has behaved overall, but your home's result will depend on its own details.

School assignment and property taxes depend on the exact address. For any home you are serious about, verify both with the school district and the county.

Nearby towns can look quite different from one another. If you are comparing, the [Franklin housing market data](/franklin/housing-market) and [Spring Hill housing market data](/spring-hill/housing-market) pages sit alongside this one. Spring Hill includes parts of both Williamson and Maury counties, so services and taxes can differ by address there.
