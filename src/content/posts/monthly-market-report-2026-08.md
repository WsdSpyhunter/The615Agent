**How to read it:** each rating blends Zillow's Market Temperature Index, Redfin's months of supply and Realtor.com's county data. Blended from up to three sources: Zillow's Market Temperature Index (55%), Redfin months of supply (30%) and Realtor.com county hotness (15%).

## Franklin: Balanced market (slightly toward sellers)

*Zillow Market Temperature Index: Neutral | Redfin months of supply: Seller's | Realtor.com Williamson County: Cool | Trend: cooling | The sources disagree*

As of August 31, 2026, Franklin is a balanced market tilting slightly toward sellers, though the sources disagree noticeably. Zillow's index reads Neutral at 46, Redfin shows 3.4 months of supply (Seller's), and Realtor.com rates Williamson County Cool at 41.7 of 100. Zillow's index has moved down 6 points over three months, and the median sale price is $863,000.

- Median sale price: $863,000 (+12.8% vs. a year earlier)
- Median days on market: 49
- Average sale-to-list ratio: 98.3%
- Active listings: 530
