Here is where the market stands along the I-65 corridor and in greater Nashville, based on data through September 30, 2026.

The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate was **7.40%** (week of October 8, 2026).

**How to read it:** each rating blends Zillow's Market Temperature Index, Redfin's months of supply and Realtor.com's county data. Blended from up to three sources: Zillow's Market Temperature Index (55%), Redfin months of supply (30%) and Realtor.com county hotness (15%).

## Franklin: Balanced market

*Zillow Market Temperature Index: Neutral | Redfin months of supply: Balanced | Realtor.com Williamson County: Cool | Trend: cooling*

Franklin is rated a balanced market as of September 30, 2026, based on rolling 3-month figures. Zillow's index is 46 (neutral) and Redfin shows 4.0 months of supply (balanced), while Realtor.com rates Williamson County 41.7 of 100 on hotness (cool), so the sources differ slightly. The median sale price is $863,089, up 12.8% from a year earlier, and Zillow's index is down 6 points over three months.

- Median sale price: $863,089 (+12.8% vs. a year earlier)
- Median days on market: 55
- Average sale-to-list ratio: 98.1%
- Active listings: 569
