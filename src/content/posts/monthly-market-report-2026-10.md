Here is where the market stands along the I-65 corridor and in greater Nashville, based on data through October 31, 2026.

The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate was **7.18%** (week of November 5, 2026).

**How to read it:** each rating blends Zillow's Market Temperature Index, Redfin's months of supply and Realtor.com's county data. Blended from up to three sources: Zillow's Market Temperature Index (55%), Redfin months of supply (30%) and Realtor.com county hotness (15%).

## Franklin: Leans toward buyers

*Zillow Market Temperature Index: Buyer's | Redfin months of supply: Balanced | Realtor.com Williamson County: Cool | Trend: steady | The sources disagree*

As of October 31, 2026, Franklin's market leans toward buyers, though the sources disagree. Zillow's Market Temperature Index is 31 (Buyer's), Redfin's months of supply is 4.1 (Balanced), and Realtor.com's Williamson County hotness score is 40.5 of 100 (Cool). The median sale price is $849,409, up 5.5% from a year earlier, with a median of 61 days on market.

- Median sale price: $849,409 (+5.5% vs. a year earlier)
- Median days on market: 61
- Average sale-to-list ratio: 97.9%
- Active listings: 538

## Brentwood: Buyer's market
