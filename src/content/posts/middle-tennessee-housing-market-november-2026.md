Prices, days on market and months of supply each tell part of the story, and they differ a lot from one city to the next. Here is where the Middle Tennessee housing market stands in late November 2026, using public data. The goal is to help you compare your own city instead of leaning on a national headline.

## The metro view and mortgage rates

![Photo: Home, for sale, house, estate, sale](/img/blog/middle-tennessee-housing-market-november-2026/inline-1.jpg)

*Photo by [paulbr75](https://pixabay.com/users/paulbr75-2938186/) on [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com)*

Start with borrowing costs. The 30-year fixed mortgage rate was 7.28% for the week of 2026-11-19, according to Freddie Mac via FRED. Nobody can reliably predict where rates go from here, so it helps to look at what a payment looks like today. The [mortgage calculator](/mortgage-calculator) lets you try your own numbers.

For the Nashville metro area, [Redfin Data Center](https://www.redfin.com/news/data-center/) shows these figures for the 4 weeks ending 2026-11-15:

- Median sale price: $471,794
- Median days on market: 68
- Sale-to-list ratio: 97.8%
- Homes sold: 2,121
- Months of supply: 6.2

Months of supply is how long it would take to sell every active listing at the current pace of sales. Under 4 months favors sellers, 4 to 6 months is balanced, and over 6 months favors buyers. At 6.2, the metro sits just above that line.

## Williamson County cities

The numbers below are from Redfin, rolling 3 months, with data as of 2026-10-31. You can see the full picture on our [market data page](/market-data).

**Franklin** had a median sale price of $849,409, up 5.5% from a year earlier. Median days on market was 61, with 538 active listings and 4.1 months of supply. That is right at the low edge of the balanced range. More detail is on the [Franklin data page](/franklin/housing-market).

**Brentwood** had a median sale price of $1,281,609, down 4.1% from a year earlier, with 5.6 months of supply and 328 active listings. The average sale-to-list ratio was 96.8%. See the [Brentwood page](/brentwood/housing-market).

**Thompson's Station** had a median sale price of $852,937, down 5.2% from a year earlier. Median days on market was 83, the longest of any city in this post, and months of supply was 5.6. The average sale-to-list ratio was 98.7%. Details are on the [Thompson's Station page](/thompsons-station/housing-market).

**Nolensville** stands out. Months of supply was 2.9 across 73 active listings, which is under the 4-month line that favors sellers. The median sale price was $969,326, down 8.6% from a year earlier. Zillow Research's Market Temperature Index moved +11 over 3 months in the data we have. See the [Nolensville page](/nolensville/housing-market).

## Spring Hill, Columbia, Murfreesboro and Nashville

![Photo: Real estate, homeownership, homebuying, home, house](/img/blog/middle-tennessee-housing-market-november-2026/inline-2.jpg)

*Photo by [OleksandrPidvalnyi](https://pixabay.com/users/OleksandrPidvalnyi-4638469/) on [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com)*

These cities sit in different counties, and that matters. Spring Hill includes parts of both Williamson and Maury counties, so services, schools and taxes can differ by address. Murfreesboro is in Rutherford County and Columbia is in Maury County. Neither is in Williamson County.

**Spring Hill:** median sale price $501,321, down 9.7% from a year earlier, with 4.5 months of supply and 420 active listings. Median days on market was 67. See the [Spring Hill page](/spring-hill/housing-market).

**Columbia:** median sale price $354,753, down 1.4% from a year earlier. Months of supply was 5.0 and median days on market was 75. The Zillow index moved +14 over 3 months. See the [Columbia page](/columbia/housing-market).

**Murfreesboro:** median sale price $426,703, down 2.5% from a year earlier, with 859 active listings and 4.5 months of supply. The average sale-to-list ratio was 98.7%. The Zillow index moved -4 over 3 months. See the [Murfreesboro page](/murfreesboro/housing-market).

**Nashville:** median sale price $457,132, down 3.8% from a year earlier. With 5,175 active listings and 6.5 months of supply, it has the highest supply figure here. See the [Nashville page](/nashville/housing-market).

Trend readings are on Zillow Research and [Realtor.com Research](https://www.realtor.com/research/data/). Realtor.com's county hotness scores were 44.7 of 100 for Williamson County, 36.3 for Maury County, 33.4 for Rutherford County and 21.2 for Davidson County.

## What the numbers can and cannot tell you

In every city above, the average sale-to-list ratio is below 100%. That means the average home sold for a little under its list price. Averages hide a lot, though. Price range, condition and the specific street can all change the result, so treat these figures as a starting point.

If you are buying, you can ask about negotiating, including asking the seller to pay some closing costs or fund a temporary or permanent rate buydown. Whether that is available depends on the seller, the home and the market, and a lender has to confirm what is allowed for your loan. Closing costs typically run 2 to 4 percent of the purchase price, plus earnest money and moving costs.

If you are selling, the local numbers are the ones to watch: months of supply, days on market and sale-to-list ratio for your own city. They can differ sharply even between neighboring cities, as Nolensville and Brentwood show.

For schools and property taxes, details depend on the exact address. Check with the school district and the county for any home you are serious about.
