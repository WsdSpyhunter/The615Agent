A short trip can answer questions a listing page cannot. How does the drive actually feel? Does a town fit your routine? Do the numbers you read online match what you see in person? If you plan to visit Nashville before moving, a little structure helps you come home with answers instead of a camera roll.

This post covers how I would set up a trip, what to check on the ground, and which numbers to look up alongside it.

## Decide What the Trip Needs to Answer

![Photo: House, spring, flower background, front door, front yard](/img/blog/visit-nashville-before-moving/inline-1.jpg)

*Photo by [emetzner130](https://pixabay.com/users/emetzner130-1429351/) on [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com)*

Start with three or four questions, not a list of places. Which towns are you comparing? Which daily trips matter most? What do you need to see in person before you narrow the list?

Middle Tennessee is a group of separate cities and counties, and the lines matter. Spring Hill includes parts of both Williamson and Maury counties, so county services, schools and taxes can differ by address. Murfreesboro is in Rutherford County and Columbia is in Maury County. Neither is in Williamson County. Note the county for each town on your list before you go.

If you plan to tour homes while you are here, ask a lender about pre-approval first. A pre-approval means the lender has reviewed your documents and credit. It is not a final loan commitment. You can also keep the [free moving checklists](/checklists) open next to your notes.

## Drive the Routes You Would Actually Use

A map flatters a commute more than anything else does. If you know where you will work, or where family lives, drive that route at the hour you would normally drive it. Do it more than once if your schedule allows.

For context, the U.S. Census Bureau's American Community Survey 5-year estimates (2020 to 2024) put mean travel time to work in Franklin at 23.9 minutes. That is an average across all workers, so your own number could be quite different.

Our pages on [Franklin commute times](/franklin/commute) and [Nashville commute times](/nashville/commute) are there for comparison. The [TDOT SmartWay traffic](https://smartway.tn.gov) site is an official source you can check during the trip as well.

## Plan the Days Before You Go

![Photo: Suburbs, homes, neighbors, neighborhood, suburbia](/img/blog/visit-nashville-before-moving/inline-2.jpg)

*Photo by [PublicCo](https://pixabay.com/users/PublicCo-5009832/) on [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com)*

Treat the trip like a work trip with some room in it. Group your stops by area so you are not crossing the region several times a day. Leave open time to spend an ordinary hour somewhere, like a grocery store or a park, so you see how a place feels on a normal afternoon.

I also own and run NashvilleWhatToDo.com, a free Nashville visitor guide, and as of November 17, 2026 it has a [free day planner](https://www.nashvillewhattodo.com/plan/), [ready-made itineraries for 1 to 3 days](https://www.nashvillewhattodo.com/itineraries/), a crowd calendar, and day-trip pages sorted by drive time from Nashville, including a Franklin page that lists Franklin at about 30 minutes from Nashville.

If you add a day trip to Franklin, check traffic for the hours you will be traveling, since drive times change through the day. Our [Franklin area guide](/areas/franklin) is a starting point for reading up before you arrive.

## Put Local Numbers Next to What You See

Impressions are useful, but they are one data point. Local numbers give you another.

Redfin Data Center reports these figures for the Nashville metro area, for the 4 weeks ending November 8, 2026: median sale price $467,332, median days on market 69, sale-to-list ratio 97.7%, months of supply 5.9, and 2,232 homes sold. Months of supply is how long it would take to sell every active listing at the current pace of sales. Under 4 months favors sellers, 4 to 6 months is balanced, and over 6 months favors buyers.

For Franklin, Redfin's rolling 3-month data as of October 31, 2026 shows a median sale price of $849,409, up 5.5% from a year earlier, median days on market of 61, and 4.1 months of supply. Conditions differ between cities and price ranges, so local data tells you more than national headlines.

On rates, Freddie Mac via FRED reported a 30-year fixed mortgage rate of 7.11% for the week of November 12, 2026. You can try your own figures in the [mortgage calculator](/mortgage-calculator).

Schools and taxes depend on the exact address. Franklin addresses are served by two public school districts, and which one applies depends on the home. Use each district's own lookup tool: [Franklin Special School District](https://www.fssd.org) and [Williamson County Schools](https://www.wcs.edu). Our [Franklin schools and zoning](/franklin/schools) page is another place to start.

The Williamson County 2026 property tax rates (county sheet) list a combined Franklin rate of $2.2433 per $100 of assessed value inside the Franklin Special School District, and $1.5660 outside it. Tennessee assesses residential property at 25% of appraised value. Rates are set each year, so check the county for updates.
