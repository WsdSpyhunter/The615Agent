If you are moving to Columbia TN, you are probably comparing it with where you live now. That might be Nashville, another part of Middle Tennessee, or another state entirely. This guide covers what I would research first: local market numbers, how taxes and schools work in Maury County, what financing looks like, and the logistics of the move itself.

For a wider look at the city, see the [Columbia real estate guide](/columbia).

## Start With Local Numbers

National headlines rarely match what is happening in one city. Local data such as months of supply, days on market and sale-to-list ratio gives a clearer picture.

Here is where Columbia stood in Redfin's rolling 3-month data as of 2026-10-31:

- Median sale price: $354,753, down 1.4% from a year earlier
- Median days on market: 75
- Average sale-to-list ratio: 96.8%
- Active listings: 450
- Months of supply: 5.0

Months of supply is how long it would take to sell every active listing at the current pace of sales. Under 4 months favors sellers, 4 to 6 months is balanced, and over 6 months favors buyers. At 5.0, Columbia sits in the balanced range.

For context, Redfin reported the Nashville metro area for the 4 weeks ending 2026-11-08 at a median sale price of $467,332, 69 median days on market, a 97.7% sale-to-list ratio and 5.9 months of supply. That is a different area and a different time window, so treat it as background and not a direct comparison. None of these numbers predicts what prices will do next.

You can see more regional figures on the [market data for the I-65 corridor](/market-data) page.

## Taxes, Schools and County Lines

Taxes and school assignment depend on the exact address, so check both for any home you get serious about.

For the 2025 tax year, the combined property tax rate inside Columbia city limits was $2.7351 per $100 of assessed value, made up of a county rate of $1.91 and a city rate of $0.8251. That comes from the Maury County community profile for Columbia, which cites the Tennessee Comptroller, Division of Property Assessments. Tennessee assesses residential property at 25% of appraised value, so the rate applies to that assessed amount and not the full price.

These are the 2025 rates, the latest I found. Maury County is in a 2026 reappraisal year, when rates are reset, so the 2026 rates may be lower. Confirm current figures with the Maury County Trustee through [Maury County Government](https://www.maurycounty-tn.gov), and use the [mortgage calculator](/mortgage-calculator) to see how taxes fit into a monthly payment.

Columbia addresses are zoned through Maury County Public Schools. The most reliable way to find the zoned school for a home is the district's own lookup tool, which you can reach through [Maury County Public Schools](https://www.mauryk12.org). My [Columbia school districts](/columbia/schools) page is another starting point.

County lines matter nearby too. Spring Hill includes parts of both Williamson and Maury counties, so services, schools and taxes can differ by address. Murfreesboro is in Rutherford County, and Columbia is in Maury County. Neither is in Williamson County. If you are weighing nearby cities, see [Columbia vs Spring Hill](/columbia/columbia-vs-spring-hill).

## Budget the Financing Before You Shop

The 30-year fixed mortgage rate averaged 7.11% in the week of 2026-11-12, according to Freddie Mac via FRED. Nobody can reliably predict where rates go from here. Many buyers wait for a drop, but waiting is a bet in either direction, and the cost of waiting is real even if it is easy to overlook. Some buyers plan to refinance later, but that is a possibility and not a plan. It depends on rates, credit, home value and income at that time, and it has its own closing costs.

A few basics on the process:

- A pre-approval means a lender has reviewed your documents and credit. A pre-qualification is a quick estimate based on what you tell the lender. Neither is a final loan commitment.
- After you apply, a lender must give you a Loan Estimate within three business days. It is a standard three-page form, so you can compare lenders side by side. The CFPB encourages getting estimates from more than one lender.
- Closing costs typically run 2 to 4 percent of the purchase price, plus earnest money and moving costs.
- The Closing Disclosure must arrive at least 3 business days before closing. Compare it with your Loan Estimate and ask questions.

You choose your own lender. Ask any lender you are considering whether they take part in Tennessee Housing Development Agency (THDA) programs. Until closing, avoid opening new credit, financing furniture or changing jobs.

If you are renting now, compare the full monthly cost of owning (payment, taxes, insurance, maintenance) with renting a comparable home. The Census Bureau's American Community Survey 5-year estimates for 2020 to 2024 put Columbia's median gross rent at $1,197 per month. For more on budgeting, see the [Columbia cost of living](/columbia/cost-of-living) page.

## Handle the Move and the Distance

If you are buying from out of state, a few things deserve extra care. Wire fraud is real. Verify wire instructions by phone using a number you already trust, and never rely on emailed instructions.

On inspections, termite and wood-destroying organism (WDO) inspections are commonly requested by buyers and lenders in Tennessee. Radon testing and a sewer scope are common add-ons to a standard home inspection. Sellers complete the residential property condition disclosure (or an exemption), and the lead-based paint disclosure applies to homes built before 1978.

For the move itself, request quotes from at least 3 licensed moving companies and book early for month-end dates. Cancel homeowner's insurance on a sold home only after closing is confirmed. Confirm your electric provider for the specific address and set up power early, and check city services through the [City of Columbia](https://www.columbiatn.gov). The [free moving checklists](/checklists) can help you keep track.

Commuting is worth testing yourself. The Census Bureau's 2020 to 2024 estimates show a mean travel time to work of 28 minutes for Columbia. Your own drive depends on where you work and what time you leave, so see [Columbia commute times](/columbia/commute) and check live conditions on [TDOT SmartWay traffic](https://smartway.tn.gov).
